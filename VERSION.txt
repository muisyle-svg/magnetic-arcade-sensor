CHAOSHEIST - PRODUCTION BUILD

Build family: Story/Normal Mode with Ring Power
Version: 1.0.0
Update: 2026-08-25

Latest change: the final Story Mode power failure now unfolds over about ten
seconds with staged electrical audio, escalating flicker/glitches, and a
classic CRT collapse from a full-screen line to a dot before black. The
seven-sensor firmware has the matching long red LED failure sequence.

Includes Windows-native ring input on joystick button 10, synchronized
low-overhead cinematic playback, the graphical seven-segment Master Emerald
power meter, Normal Mode return sounds, safer Ring Power game handoff, a
confirmed ring-count reset, and safe ring-total announcements over Big Box or
the guard screen, never over a running emulator. This reliability revision
adds validated serial heartbeats, fixed seven-sensor configuration checks,
configurable emulator names, ring-counter backup recovery, and durable pending
50-ring prize announcements.

The bulletproofing pass fixes Ring Power foreground recursion, restores the
Normal Mode all-missing lock after a paid game, removes every small overlay as
soon as an emulator appears, resumes Big Box before audio cleanup, defers mode
activation until prior cleanup is complete, re-queues interrupted prize
messages, supervises ring input, makes serial-worker death explicit, and adds
deadlines for cinematic and victory callbacks that could otherwise wait
forever.

This follow-up adds extra bottom safety space to meter announcements and lets
any joystick button or direction dismiss temporary emerald stolen/restored
messages.

The final Story Mode emerald removal now announces zero Chaos Energy with the
segmented meter at 0, plays the final-emerald voice line immediately, and then
uses a long image-free simulated power-loss sequence over the frozen Big Box
menu before the existing heist narration begins. The menu remains visible
through the flashes and dimming until the final blackout. Robotnik's lock
screen remains reserved for after the Sonic cinematic. The seven-sensor
firmware mirrors that moment with a final red LED failure effect, then returns
to its normal red warning pulse.

The first shutdown narration card now plays
i-m-afraid-our-little-game-ends-now.mp3.

The power-loss presentation now continues in visual-only mode if Big Box
suspension or background-audio muting is unavailable, and retries loading the
shutdown voice clip when the narration card is reached.

This update adds ring-entry audio, immediate live updates to an active ring
counter banner, vertically fitted announcement windows, a Normal Mode
all-emeralds-missing lock that still accepts rings, a Normal Mode Emerald
Restored announcement with current energy, and the Medallion prize message.
The 50-ring milestone plays the optional act-clear sound when
act-clear.mp3 (or a descriptive act/clear audio filename) is available.

This update adds the global Ctrl+Alt+F9 Story Mode testing shortcut, which
skips the Sonic CD cinematic while preserving the heist narration and
Robotnik lock screen.

This update corrects the Normal Mode zero-energy display, adds the graphical
segmented Master Emerald meter to the Normal Mode theft/restoration banners,
makes the 50-ring prize interrupt ordinary ring-count banners immediately,
and changes the Story Mode Robotnik footer to explain that a ring is needed
until full energy returns.

The segmented Master Emerald meter now also appears on Story Mode emerald
stolen and emerald restored announcements.

This reliability update keeps the 50-ring Medallion prize as the highest
priority presentation: later rings and emerald transitions are deferred,
ordinary event sounds cannot replace its act-clear audio, and its display
timer is not reset by additional rings. Regular ring notices are shorter,
dismissible with any joystick button or direction, and Story Mode keeps the
ring-required footer on every Robotnik missing-emerald state.

This production promotion renames the application, executable, source,
configuration, build output, logs, and persistent-data directory to
ChaosHeist. Existing ring totals are validated and migrated exactly once from
the former local app-data directory. ChaosHeist deliberately keeps both its
new single-instance lock and the former guard lock so old and new executables
cannot run together. Superseded local builds are preserved in the dated
pre-ChaosHeist archive.

The production reliability pass adds malformed-config fail-open diagnostics,
unknown-setting warnings, explicit ring-state version checks, recovery from an
interrupted atomic save, previous-generation backups, serialized/coalesced
background persistence for rapid rings, cross-encoder ring debounce, a final
shutdown flush, exact build-environment checks, deterministic media/Tk
preflight, Windows version metadata, packaged self-test, versioned release
archives, and SHA-256 manifests.

The final adversarial pass also makes a silent controller time out during
activation, ends an unused Normal Mode Ring Power pass when any Chaos Energy
returns, rejects stale takeover callbacks after deactivation, and aborts the
power-loss sequence fail-open if Big Box cannot be paused or muted safely.
The topmost watchdog also keeps the black application root hidden throughout
the menu-backed CRT failure effect instead of being able to cover that effect.
Small announcements are now forcibly removed when an emulator takes the
foreground; an interrupted 50-ring prize is saved and replayed in full at the
next safe Big Box menu.
Normal Mode also suppresses the emerald-return sound when its corresponding
safe-menu notice cannot be displayed, preventing shrine effects over games.
